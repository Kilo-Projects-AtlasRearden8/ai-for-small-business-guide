# AI for Small Businesses and LLCs — Manual Index

Welcome. This manual is written in plain English, step by step, the way a
friend would explain it. You do not need a technical background. Everything
here uses free AI models, and nothing here asks you to paste real customer or
financial data into an AI tool.

## Who this is for

This guide was built for **Asdale Dynamics, LLC** (Indiana, formed October
2026), founded by Dylan Asdale — a registered nurse turned AI author. It is for
small business owners, LLC owners, side hustlers, authors, and anyone who runs
the admin side of a business and wants practical help without paying for
expensive software.

It supports **The Massive AI Manual** by Dylan Asdale and the hub site
**dylanasdale.github.io**, where AI is explained "like a friend would."

## Golden rules

1. **Never paste real customer names, addresses, payment details, or tax numbers
   into an AI tool.** Use fake examples instead.
2. **AI is a helper, not an accountant or a lawyer.** Always double-check math
   and legal paperwork yourself.
3. **Start small.** One task, one chat, one free model. Build from there.
4. **Keep a human in the loop.** You read and approve everything before it goes
   to a customer or the government.

## How to use this manual

Read in order if you are new to AI. Jump to any section if you already know the
basics. Every chapter has step-by-step instructions and an "Try this now"
exercise. Print the planners in Chapter 9 to work away from the screen.
