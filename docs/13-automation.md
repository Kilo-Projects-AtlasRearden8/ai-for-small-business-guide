# Automation

Automation means having apps do repetitive tasks for you. You do not need to
code much — many tools connect apps with clicks.

## Simple automation ideas for a small LLC

- Save email attachments to a folder automatically
- Post the same message to Instagram and Facebook at once
- Create a calendar event when a customer books something
- Send a receipt by email after a sale

## No-code tools (free tiers)

Tools like Zapier, Make, and IFTTT offer free tiers with limited tasks per
month. Check their current pricing before you connect anything.

## Step-by-step: your first automation (example)

1. Pick a free automation tool and create an account.
2. Choose a "trigger" (an event that starts the automation), such as "new form
   submission."
3. Choose an "action" (what happens next), such as "send an email" or "add a
   row to a spreadsheet."
4. Connect your accounts. Use strong, unique passwords.
5. Test with fake data first. Do not use real customer information.
6. Turn it on and check it weekly.

## What to avoid

- Do not connect automation to your bank account unless you understand the
  security guarantees.
- Do not automate emails to real customers until you have read and approved
  the drafts.
- Do not paste real customer or financial data into automation setup forms
  beyond what the app legitimately needs.
