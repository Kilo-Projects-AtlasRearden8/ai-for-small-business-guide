# Healthcare-Worker AI Aids (Education Only)

Dylan Asdale is a registered nurse, and Asdale Dynamics, LLC creates
education-only tools for healthcare workers. This chapter explains what those
tools are for — and what they are not.

## Important: not medical advice

Everything in this chapter is for **education, organization, and paperwork
support only**. It is not medical advice, not a diagnosis, not a treatment
recommendation, and not a substitute for a licensed clinician.

If you or someone else needs medical care, contact a qualified healthcare
professional or emergency services.

## What AI can help with in healthcare work

- Explaining medical terms in plain language for patients
- Drafting patient education handouts (to be reviewed by a clinician)
- Organizing shift notes and continuing-education logs
- Drafting emails to colleagues or administrators
- Summarizing public health guidelines (always check the original source)

## What AI should not do in healthcare

- Diagnose conditions
- Recommend medications or dosages
- Interpret lab results for individual patients
- Write chart entries for real patients
- Store real patient information

## Privacy in healthcare

Healthcare data is protected by law (for example, HIPAA in the United States).
**Never paste real patient names, dates of birth, medical record numbers, or
clinical details into an AI tool.** Use fake case examples only.

## Try this now (education only)

Ask a free AI chat:

> "Explain the term 'hypertension' in plain English, as if I were explaining it
> to a patient. Include when to call a doctor. Add a disclaimer that this is
> not medical advice."

Review the output with a clinician before sharing it with anyone.
