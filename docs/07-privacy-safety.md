# Privacy and Safety

## The most important rule

**Never paste real customer or financial data into an AI tool.**

That means no:
- Real customer names, addresses, phone numbers, or email addresses
- Order numbers, invoice numbers, or tracking numbers
- Credit card numbers, bank account numbers, or payment details
- Tax IDs, Social Security numbers, or EINs
- Real profit and loss figures from your business
- Employee or contractor personal information

If you want help drafting something, rewrite it with fake details first:

| Real | Fake version to paste into AI |
|------|------------------------------|
| Jane Doe, 123 Main St, Indianapolis | Customer A, 123 Example St, Example City |
| $4,820.50 profit last month | About $5,000 profit last month |
| Order #INV-88421 | Order #EXAMPLE-0001 |

Then add the real details yourself in your own email, spreadsheet, or
accounting program.

## Why this matters

- AI chats may be stored or reviewed by the company that runs them.
- Once data is pasted, you cannot take it back.
- Small businesses are real targets for scams and data theft.
- LLCs have legal duties to protect customer information.

## How to use AI safely

1. **Sanitize before you paste.** Remove names, numbers, and details. Use
   placeholders like [CUSTOMER NAME] and [AMOUNT].
2. **Check the output.** AI can invent laws, prices, and policies. Verify
   anything that affects money or customers.
3. **Keep business and personal separate.** Do not paste personal health or
   family information either.
4. **Use strong passwords** on your AI accounts, and turn on two-factor
   authentication if available.
5. **Log out** when you are done, especially on shared computers.

## If something goes wrong

- If you accidentally paste real data, delete the chat if the service allows
  it, change any exposed passwords, and consider notifying affected people.
- If AI gives you wrong tax or legal information, fix it before you file or
  sign anything. When in doubt, talk to an accountant or lawyer.
