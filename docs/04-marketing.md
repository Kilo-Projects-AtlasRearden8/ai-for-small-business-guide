# Marketing Help with AI

AI can help you write marketing copy, but your voice and your customers matter
more than anything the AI generates. Use AI for drafts and ideas, then edit
until it sounds like you.

## 1. Social media posts

Example prompt:

> "I run a small candle-making LLC. Write 5 short Instagram captions for a new
> autumn-scented candle line. Each caption under 60 words, friendly tone,
> including a call to action like 'Shop the link in bio.' Use emojis sparingly."

Then pick the ones you like, add your photo, and post.

## 2. Product descriptions

If you sell on a marketplace (Etsy, eBay, Amazon, your own site), AI can draft
descriptions from rough notes.

Example:

> "Write a product description for a hand-poured soy candle, 8 ounces, scent
> called 'Maple Morning,' burn time 45 hours. Tone: warm and cozy. Include
> bullet points for features and a short safety note about never leaving a
> burning candle unattended."

## 3. Flyers and print materials

AI cannot design images for you in this manual (we stick to free text models),
but it can write the text for flyers, business cards, and posters.

Example:

> "Write the text for a half-page flyer for a mobile dog-grooming business
> serving [town]. Include services, prices, phone number, and a line that says
> 'First-time clients get 10% off.' Keep it short enough to print at home."

Replace [town] with your real town — that is public business info, not private
customer data.

## 4. Ad copy and promotions

> "Write 3 short Facebook ad headlines and 3 short bodies for a weekend sale at
> my vintage clothing shop. Under 30 words each, enthusiastic but not hype-y."

## 5. Repurpose content

Take one piece of writing and ask AI to turn it into others:

> "Turn this product description into an email subject line, an Instagram
> caption, and a text-message version for loyal customers."

[Paste your description with private details removed.]

## Do's and don'ts

Do:
- Use AI for first drafts and brainstorming
- Add your own photos, prices, and policies
- Read everything out loud before publishing
- Keep a list of what worked so you can do more of it

Don't:
- Paste customer lists or purchase histories into AI
- Let AI write pricing you have not verified
- Publish AI text without reading it (it can sound generic or make false
  claims)
