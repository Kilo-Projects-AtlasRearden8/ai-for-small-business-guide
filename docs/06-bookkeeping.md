# Bookkeeping Help with AI

AI can help you organize and understand your bookkeeping, but it is **not a
replacement for real bookkeeping software or a tax professional**. Use AI for
drafts, categories, and explanations — keep your official records in a
spreadsheet or accounting program, and never paste real bank statements or tax
numbers into AI.

## What AI can help with

- Explaining bookkeeping terms in plain English
- Suggesting expense categories for your LLC
- Drafting invoice templates
- Creating checklists for tax preparation
- Turning a list of expenses into a simple summary (with fake numbers)

## What AI should not do

- File your taxes
- Calculate your actual tax bill
- Access your bank account
- Store your real receipts or financial records
- Give tax or legal advice

## Expense categories for a small LLC

Common categories you can use in your own spreadsheet:

- Advertising and marketing
- Office supplies
- Software and subscriptions
- Shipping and postage
- Professional services (legal, accounting)
- Utilities and internet
- Travel and meals (business only)
- Inventory and materials
- Taxes and licenses

## Step-by-step: categorizing expenses safely

1. Export your transactions from your bank as a CSV or print them out.
2. In a notebook or spreadsheet, write: Date | Description | Category | Amount.
3. If you want AI help, copy **only** the merchant names (e.g. "Staples,"
   "USPS," "Amazon") into an AI chat — no amounts, no account numbers, no
   customer names.
4. Ask: "Which bookkeeping category should I use for Staples, USPS, and Amazon
   for a small home-office LLC?"
5. Apply the categories yourself in your real spreadsheet.

## Tax preparation checklist

AI can draft a checklist like this; you fill it in with real records:

- [ ] All invoices sent this year are saved
- [ ] All business receipts are saved (photos or paper)
- [ ] Bank statements for the business account are downloaded
- [ ] Mileage log is complete (if you drive for business)
- [ ] Home office expenses are tallied (if applicable)
- [ ] 1099 forms received are filed
- [ ] Tax appointment is scheduled
