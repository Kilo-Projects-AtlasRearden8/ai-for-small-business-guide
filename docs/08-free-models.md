# Free AI Models

You do not need to pay for AI to run a small business. There are free models
and free tiers you can use today without a credit card.

## Where to find free AI

### Free web chat services
Many AI companies offer a free chat tier in a browser or phone app. You
typically need to create an account, but you do not need to pay.

Examples (free tiers, as of this writing):
- ChatGPT free tier (OpenAI)
- Claude free tier (Anthropic)
- Gemini free tier (Google)
- Copilot (Microsoft)

Check each site's current pricing before you rely on it. Free tiers often have
limits on how many messages you can send per day.

### Open weights models you can download
If you want to run AI on your own computer (offline, private), see the Local AI
chapter. Free models you can download include:
- Llama family (Meta)
- Qwen (Alibaba)
- Mistral (Mistral AI)
- Gemma (Google)

## How to choose

| If you want... | Try... |
|----------------|--------|
| The easiest start, no install | A free web chat tier |
| Privacy and offline use | Local AI (download a model) |
| No account at all | Some demo pages (varies by site) |
| To build tools or bots | Free API tiers with usage limits (be careful — free tiers can still require a payment method on file) |

## Before you sign up

- Use an email address dedicated to your business, not your personal one, if
  you prefer.
- Turn on two-factor authentication if the service offers it.
- Read the privacy notice briefly so you know whether your chats are stored or
  used for training. Many services let you opt out in settings.
- **Still never paste real customer or financial data** (see Privacy and
  Safety).

## Try this now

Open one free web chat and send this message:

> "I run a small business. Can you help me draft a polite price-increase
> notice for customers? Give me a template with [brackets] for the parts I
> fill in myself."
