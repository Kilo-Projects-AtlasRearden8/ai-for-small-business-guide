Golden rules

  • Never paste real customer names, addresses, payment details, or tax numbers into an AI tool. Use fake examples instead.
  • AI is a helper, not an accountant or a lawyer. Always double-check math and legal paperwork yourself.
  • Start small. One task, one chat, one free model.
  • Keep a human in the loop. Read and approve everything before it goes to a customer or the government.
