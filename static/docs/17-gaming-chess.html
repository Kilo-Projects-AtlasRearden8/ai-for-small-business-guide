Fun Extras: FFXIV and Chess

This project also includes small, fun tools for gaming and chess. These are for personal enjoyment, not business.

FFXIV (Final Fantasy XIV)

AI can help with:

  • Explaining game mechanics in plain language
  • Drafting macro text (copy into the game yourself)
  • Planning crafting rotations (verify in-game)
  • Writing roleplay dialogue for your character

Never share your account password or real-world personal information in game chats or AI tools.

Chess

AI can help with:

  • Explaining openings and tactics in plain language
  • Suggesting puzzles to practice
  • Analyzing a game you played (paste moves only, not personal details)

Use free chess sites like Lichess or Chess.com for playing and puzzles.
