Customer Emails with AI

Email is often the first impression a customer has of your business. AI can draft emails quickly, but you must read and personalize every one before you send.

Safety rule for customer emails

Never paste a real customer's email into an AI tool if it contains their name, address, phone number, order details, or payment information. Rewrite the situation with fake details first, then add the real details yourself in your email program.

Email templates

Below are fill-in-the-blank templates. Replace bracketed text with your own details. Do not send real customer data through AI.

Welcome email (new customer)

Subject: Welcome to [Business Name]

Hi [First Name],

Welcome to [Business Name]! I'm [Your Name], and I'm glad you're here.

We specialize in [one sentence about what you do]. If you ever have questions, just reply to this email — I answer every message personally.

Here is what to expect next: [one sentence, e.g. "Your order ships within 2 business days."]

Thanks for supporting a small business.

[Your Name] [Business Name] [Phone or website]

Appointment reminder

Subject: Reminder: your appointment on [date]

Hi [First Name],

This is a friendly reminder about your [service] appointment on [date] at [time].

Location: [address or "online via Zoom"] What to bring: [list] Cancellation: please let me know at least [24] hours ahead if you need to reschedule.

See you soon,

[Your Name] [Business Name]

Follow-up after a purchase

Subject: Thanks for your order from [Business Name]

Hi [First Name],

Thank you for your order of [item] on [date]. I hope you enjoy it!

If anything arrives damaged or you have questions, reply to this email and I will make it right.

If you have a moment, I would appreciate a review: [review link]

Thanks again,

[Your Name] [Business Name]

Late-payment reminder (polite)

Subject: Friendly reminder: invoice [number] from [Business Name]

Hi [First Name],

I'm writing to remind you that invoice [number] for [amount] was due on [due date]. If you've already sent the payment, please disregard this message.

You can pay here: [payment link]

Thanks,

[Your Name] [Business Name]

General reply template

Subject: Re: [original subject]

Hi [First Name],

Thanks for reaching out. [One sentence answering their main question.]

[One sentence with next steps or a question back to them.]

Best,

[Your Name] [Business Name]

Step-by-step: sending an email with AI help

  1. Open your email program (not the AI).
  2. Start a new draft and fill in the customer's real address yourself.
  3. If you want AI help, write a fake version of the customer's message in a

separate note, with names and details changed.

  1. Paste the fake version into an AI chat and ask for a draft reply.
  2. Copy the AI draft into your email draft.
  3. Add real details, read it out loud, fix anything that sounds wrong, then

send.
