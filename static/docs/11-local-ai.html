Local AI

Local AI means running an AI model on your own computer, instead of sending messages to a company's website. Nothing you type leaves your machine.

Why local AI matters for a small business

  • Privacy: your drafts and notes stay on your computer
  • Offline: works without internet
  • Free: no subscription, no usage limits
  • Good for: sensitive drafts, brainstorming, documents you do not want uploaded

What you need

  • A computer (Windows, Mac, or Linux)
  • Enough disk space (a few gigabytes per model)
  • A free local AI app

Free local AI apps (beginner friendly)

These apps let you download and run models with a few clicks:

  • Ollama (ollama.com) — command-line friendly, simple desktop app available
  • LM Studio (lmstudio.ai) — visual interface, good for beginners
  • Jan (jan.ai) — open-source local AI desktop app

Check each site for current system requirements before downloading.

Step-by-step: your first local model (with Ollama as an example)

  1. Download Ollama from ollama.com and install it.
  2. Open a terminal (Command Prompt, PowerShell, or Terminal).
  3. Download a small model:

`` ollama run llama3.2 ``

  1. Type your message and press enter. The model replies.
  2. Type /bye to exit.

The first time you run a model, it downloads (a few minutes). After that, it works offline.

What model to start with

  • Small models (e.g., Llama 3.2 1B, Qwen 2.5 1.5B) run on most computers and

are fine for drafting emails, summaries, and ideas.

  • Larger models need more memory (RAM) and may be slow on older computers.

Safety note

Local AI is private, but your computer still needs basic security: updates, passwords, and backups. Local AI does not make its output true — check everything just like web AI.

Try this now

Install a local AI app and ask it:

"Write a one-paragraph welcome email for a new customer of a small landscaping LLC. Use a fake customer name."
