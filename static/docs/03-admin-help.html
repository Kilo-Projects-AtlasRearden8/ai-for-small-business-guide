Admin Help with AI

AI is useful for the boring admin work that eats your day: scheduling, emails, paperwork, meeting notes, and written procedures.

1. Tame your inbox

You can use AI to draft replies, but do not log into your email through an AI tool, and do not paste real customer emails containing private data.

Step by step:

  1. Copy an email you received into a note app. Remove the customer's real name,

address, phone number, and any order numbers. Replace them with fake ones.

  1. Paste the cleaned-up version into an AI chat.
  2. Ask: "Draft a polite reply to this customer. They are asking about pricing

for [service]. Keep it under 100 words and friendly."

  1. Copy the draft into your real email program, add the real details yourself,

read it, and send.

2. Meeting notes and summaries

After a meeting, type your rough notes into AI:

"Here are rough notes from a meeting with a vendor. Turn them into: - Decisions made - Action items, with who is responsible - Next steps and dates

Notes: [paste your notes, with real names removed]"

Save the result as the official meeting note in your files.

3. Standard operating procedures (SOPs)

An SOP is a written recipe for a recurring task. AI is good at turning messy notes into clean steps.

Example prompt:

"I run a small bakery LLC. Write an SOP for opening the shop each morning, as a numbered checklist. Include unlocking, lights, display case, music, and cash drawer. Keep each step to one line."

Print the SOP and post it where you work.

4. Paperwork tracker

AI cannot file your paperwork for you, but it can help you build a tracker. Use the Printable Planners chapter for paper versions you can fill in by hand.

Try this now:

"Make a simple monthly paperwork tracker for a small LLC. Include columns for date, document type, vendor, amount, and whether it is filed. Show it as a Markdown table I can copy into a notebook."
