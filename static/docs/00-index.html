Golden rules

  1. **Never paste real customer names, addresses, payment details, or tax numbers

into an AI tool.** Use fake examples instead.

  1. AI is a helper, not an accountant or a lawyer. Always double-check math

and legal paperwork yourself.

  1. Start small. One task, one chat, one free model. Build from there.
  2. Keep a human in the loop. You read and approve everything before it goes

to a customer or the government.

How to use this manual

Read in order if you are new to AI. Jump to any section if you already know the basics. Every chapter has step-by-step instructions and an "Try this now" exercise. Print the planners in Chapter 9 to work away from the screen.
