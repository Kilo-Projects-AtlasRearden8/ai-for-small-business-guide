Reselling: eBay and Amazon

If you resell items on eBay, Amazon, or similar marketplaces, AI can help with listings, pricing research notes, and customer messages.

Listing titles and descriptions

Example prompt:

"Write an eBay listing title (under 80 characters) and a 3-paragraph description for a used Nintendo Switch in good condition, including dock and cables. Tone: honest and friendly. Mention that it has been tested and works."

Then add your own photos, condition notes, and shipping details.

Pricing notes

AI can suggest how to think about pricing:

"What should I consider when pricing used video games on eBay? List 4 factors in one sentence each."

Always check recent sold listings yourself before setting a price.

Customer messages

Use the templates in the Customer Emails chapter, adapted for marketplace messaging. Never share customer addresses or order details in AI.

Inventory tracker (paper)

Use the printable planners to log what you list, where, and for how much — before you enter it in your spreadsheet or bookkeeping software.

Try this now

Ask a free AI chat:

"Write 3 short eBay message templates: one thanking a buyer, one explaining a shipping delay, and one asking for a review. Each under 50 words."
