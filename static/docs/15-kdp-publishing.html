KDP and Book Publishing

Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP) lets you publish ebooks and paperbacks for free. AI can help with the writing and marketing around publishing — but you write the book.

How AI can support publishing

  • Brainstorming book titles and subtitles
  • Writing blurbs and book descriptions
  • Drafting marketing emails and social posts for launch day
  • Creating keyword ideas for your Amazon listing
  • Planning a writing schedule

What AI should not do

  • Write an entire book for you and publish it without your review (readers and

Amazon expect original work)

  • Give legal advice about contracts or ISBNs
  • Create cover images unless you have the rights to the images used

Step-by-step: a KDP blurb with AI

  1. Write a few sentences about your book yourself.
  2. Paste them into a free AI chat (no real customer data needed).
  3. Ask:

> "Turn these notes into a 100-word Amazon book blurb for a nonfiction > book about AI for nurses. Tone: warm and encouraging."

  1. Rewrite it in your own voice. Check that all claims are true.

Try this now

Ask a free AI chat:

"Give me 5 title ideas for a beginner book about using AI in a small cleaning business. Keep titles short and friendly."
