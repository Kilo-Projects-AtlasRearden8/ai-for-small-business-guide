Getting Started with AI

What AI is, in plain English

AI (artificial intelligence) is a computer program that is good at reading and writing. You type a message, and it writes back. It can summarize long emails, draft letters, suggest marketing slogans, sort lists, and help you write code.

Think of it like a very fast intern who has read almost everything on the internet but knows nothing about your business unless you tell it. It is helpful, but it can also make things up. That is why you always check its work.

What you need to start

  • A computer, tablet, or phone with an internet connection
  • A free account at an AI chat service (see Chapter 8 for free models)
  • About 15 minutes

You do not need to install software, write code, or pay for anything.

Your first AI chat, step by step

  1. Open a free AI chat service in your browser.
  2. Create an account if asked. Use an email address you check regularly.
  3. In the message box, type something like this:

`` I run a small cleaning business. Please write a short welcome email for a new customer named Jamie. Keep it friendly and under 100 words. ``

  1. Press send. Read what the AI writes.
  2. Change anything you do not like. Add your business name, phone number, and

service area. Delete anything that sounds wrong.

  1. Copy the final version into your own email program and send it.

That is it. You just used AI.

How to write a good prompt

A prompt is just the message you send the AI. Better prompts get better answers.

Instead of...Try...
"Write an email.""Write a polite email to a customer who paid late, asking them to pay by Friday. Keep it under 80 words."
"Help with marketing.""Give me 5 Instagram caption ideas for a handmade jewelry shop launching a fall collection."
"Summarize this.""Summarize this document in 3 bullet points, written for a customer who does not know technical terms."

Tips:

  • Say who you are and what your business does.
  • Say who the reader is.
  • Say how long you want the answer to be.
  • Say the tone you want (friendly, professional, short, enthusiastic).

Try this now

Open a free AI chat and ask:

"I am starting a small business. What are 3 simple ways AI could help me this week? Keep each answer to one sentence."

Pick one and try it.

What AI cannot do well

  • It cannot see inside your bank account unless you connect tools yourself.
  • It cannot file your taxes or sign legal documents for you.
  • It cannot guarantee anything it writes is true or legal.
  • It can invent facts, prices, and laws. Always double-check.
